Optoelectronic model, uncertainty and experimental protocol
DNc ≈ Dc + g · t · ∫ E(λ) · ρ(λ) · T(λ) · Qc(λ) dλ + noise

Incident spectral irradiance E, leaf reflectance ρ, optical transmission T and detector response Q jointly determine the signal. Gain g and exposure t change the digital value. This linear expression precedes nonlinear camera processing; gamma, tone mapping and automatic controls can invalidate direct radiometry.

r = (S − D)/(W − D)   ·   ρ̂ = ρw · r

Dark/white references must match illumination, geometry, ROI, detector channel, exposure, gain and processing. A white reference does not remove leaf-angle, specular or tissue-scattering effects. Pixel flat-field ratios and a ratio of ROI means are compared as separate optical processing conditions. Out-of-range values are flagged rather than silently clipped.

For a ratio of ROI means, uncertainty uses derivatives with respect to S, W, D and (when supplied) ρw. The shared dark term is included once through ∂ρ̂/∂D = ρw(S−W)/(W−D)². Two-frame block means describe temporal variation; they do not establish independent biological replication or a complete uncertainty budget. Geometry, drift, spectral mismatch and unknown reference uncertainty remain material limitations.

Record plant/specimen IDs, source spectrum and FWHM, measured irradiance when available, working distance, angle, polariser/filter details, exposure/gain/white-balance state, reference target/certificate, temperature and acquisition order. Randomise or counterbalance physical illumination order. Re-reference after changes; use healthy, disease-confirmed and non-disease-stress plants. Keep plants separate across training and evaluation.

Separate the optical endpoint (repeatability, reference stability, response contrast) from the disease endpoint (independent reference labels and plant-level evaluation). An inference-channel ablation is not a physical wavelength ablation. No disease accuracy is inferred from unlabelled frames, response ratios or vegetation indices.

Sources: PlantCV calibration documentation · EMVA 1288 · W3C Image Capture · Tomato disease hyperspectral study.
